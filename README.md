# A Hundov Ranch 2.0

**A Hundov Ranch 2.0** is a Minecraft mod focused on making survival harsher, more demanding, and more meaningful — with **hunger as one of your main enemies**.

The mod expands the vanilla survival experience rather than replacing it. Minecraft is still Minecraft, but food, exploration, combat, trading, farming, and progression all require more planning.

## Hunger Is Your Enemy

Food is no longer something you can casually eat whenever the hunger bar gets low.

* Food restores significantly less hunger and saturation.
* The amount of hunger and saturation restored depends on the selected difficulty.
* Sprinting, jumping, swimming, mining, building, and other activities consume more exhaustion.
* Additional sources of exhaustion are introduced.
* Managing your food supply becomes an important part of survival.

Running everywhere is no longer free.

And neither is ignoring your food.

## A Harder Minecraft

A Hundov Ranch 2.0 also changes how you progress through the game.

The **Nether and the End are not available by default**. Reaching them requires completing specific achievements and obtaining special items.

Along the way, you will encounter:

* Rebalanced villager trading.
* Changes to dungeon and structure progression.
* New farming requirements.
* More demanding food management.
* Food spoilage and consequences of eating spoiled food.
* Stronger hostile mobs with additional abilities.
* Passive mobs that try to avoid the player.
* A greater emphasis on preparation before exploration.

The goal is not simply to make Minecraft harder for the sake of making it harder.

The goal is to make **preparation, production, exploration, and resource management matter**.

**Hunger is your enemy.**
Food can spoil.
A bad meal at the wrong time can become a serious problem.
And poison won't stop you from taking that final half-heart of damage. =]

## Development Status

A Hundov Ranch 2.0 is currently in active development.

The project is being rebuilt around a simple principle:

> **Build the smallest working mechanic first. Then expand it when the game proves that it needs more.**

Many systems are experimental and may change significantly during development.

Expect unfinished features, balance changes, temporary implementations, and things that may occasionally break.

## For Contributors

Contributions are welcome, especially when they help improve the actual gameplay experience.

Before working on a larger feature:

1. Check the existing issues and project direction.
2. Keep changes focused on the problem being solved.
3. Prefer vanilla Minecraft and Fabric functionality when it already fits the task.
4. Avoid introducing large abstractions or systems before they are actually needed.
5. Test gameplay changes in-game whenever possible.
6. Keep client-only and server/common code properly separated.

For larger changes, opening an issue first is recommended so the idea can be discussed before implementation.

## For Content Creators

A Hundov Ranch 2.0 is intended to be playable, recorded, streamed, reviewed, and discussed.

If you create videos, streams, articles, or other content featuring the mod, you are welcome to do so.

You may use the mod in publicly shared gameplay content without asking for individual permission.

If you encounter a bug, unexpected interaction, or interesting gameplay situation while creating content, feel free to report it through the GitHub issue tracker.

## Reporting Bugs

When reporting a bug, please include:

* Minecraft version.
* A Hundov Ranch 2.0 version or commit.
* Fabric Loader and Fabric API versions.
* A clear description of what happened.
* Steps to reproduce the issue.
* Crash logs or relevant logs, if available.
* Other installed mods, if the issue may involve compatibility.

Screenshots and short videos are especially useful for gameplay-related issues.
